# Kicker

The small label above a headline, with its meta, caption and byline companions.

## Anatomy
- Kicker: `kicker` style (Condensed 700, 12px, tracked .14em, uppercase) in `link`, led by a 22px 2px-tall `orange` dash.
- Byline: `byline` in `ink`, the date after it in `meta` weight and `ink-2`.
- Meta and caption: `meta` (Barlow 600, 12px) and `caption` (Barlow 500) in `ink-2`; the photographer's name in `ink` bold.

## Use it
- One kicker per story: "The Big Read" for any lead, "The Buddy Martin column" for his newest.
- Keep it to three words or fewer.

## Don't
- No kicker in `orange` text; the dash is the orange, the text is `link`.
