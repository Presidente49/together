# MagazineCover

The web Magazine's opening spread: an issue line, the official mast, one cover story and three cover lines, framed by 3px `navy` rules.

## Anatomy
- Issue line in `edition` (`slate-meta`, 0.12em).
- Mast: the wordmark at 240px plus "Magazine" in Barlow 800 at 30px, over a `line` hairline.
- Hero at `ratio-cover` on `photo-well` (contain), with the lead copy beside it: kicker, headline in `page-title`, a `deck` in `slate-meta`.
- Three cover lines in `card-headline`, each with a kicker naming the writer.
- One column at 820px, where the hero becomes 16/10 and at most 320px tall.

## Use it
- The Magazine is a separate destination from the free front page. Don't repeat the front-page lead as the cover unless it is Buddy Martin's latest.
- Links belong on this web layer. Never bake buttons, schedules or nav into cover artwork.
- The consumer supplies the issue label, the cover story and three cover lines.
