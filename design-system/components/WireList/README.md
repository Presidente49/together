# WireList

The front page's Latest column: a SectionTitle, a two-column grid of wire StoryCards, and "All stories →".

## Use it
- Six items, strictly newest first, excluding the lead. Supporting stories may rotate between visits, never while someone is reading.
- On desktop it sits in the right rail (at least 260px) behind a `hairline` left divider with `space-24` padding. At 820px the divider drops and the list follows the lead.
- The consumer supplies the ordered stories and the archive URL.
