What we saw on the porch

The hard part is done.

We went through backporchsports.com, the show library, YouTube, Facebook and the handles people search for. The design is finished, the partners are signed and the voices are real. What's missing is the machinery that moves it.

The show
Saturday Sessions with Scott and Ronnie runs long and unhurried. The six episodes on the shelf run 46 minutes to an hour and a half, with David Epps, Ryan Bartow, Stephen Garcia, David Cutcliffe, Connor O'Gara and Bill Bender. Chris Low of On3 took the Duncan Williams hotline in May.
The desk
Tyler Raley, Jay Douglas and Ryan Bartow of Auburn Undercover file daily: camp notes, practice video, the receiver room and a new quarterback. Ronnie also hosts Bowden Ball with Terry Bowden and Coach's Corner with Ellis Johnson.
The partners
Duck Club Bourbon presents the show. Duncan Williams Asset Management sponsors the hotline and Kalshi is in the credits. Bar 33 in Memphis, YETI and Fanatics have units built into the site.
The reach
The Chris Low episode showed 77 YouTube views when we checked. The Facebook page has 55 followers, and its latest post, Back Porch Shorts: Week 4 Morning Edition, had one reaction. The conversation is strong and the distribution is thin.
The handles
@backporchsports on Instagram and @BackporchSports on X belong to other, older shows, and we found no TikTok. Back Porch Sports has no Instagram, X or TikTok of its own yet, and those are the platforms where clips travel.

Independent, together

Two independent newsrooms and one porch between them.

Ronnie Sanders has published Auburn Undercover since 2008, and this summer he took it independent after 16 years on 247Sports. GatorBait Media is independent too. Neither one needs a network to own it. Both are better with a shared crew and a shared audience. Back Porch Sports, with Scott and Ronnie, is the SEC show at the center of that. It's a new thing, built from scratch, and every newsroom that joins keeps its own name.

Cross-newsroom reach

GatorBait's Florida audience, Auburn Undercover's members, The Buddy Martin Show and GatorBait TV all point to the porch, and the porch sends each fan back to their school's site.

Guest swaps

One production crew
