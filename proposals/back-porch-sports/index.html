What changed on the site and in the stack

Front page
Rebuilt as a free sports front page with Buddy's newest column leading and everything else newest first. It paints instantly and now serves from Wix, so no outside CDN sits in the reader's path.
Mobile
The phone layout uses the phone's real width, a two-column photo grid for Latest, and a repaired drawer. It's checked at 390px on every change.
Type and design
One type family sitewide, one design system, and a live check that fails the build if the wrong font shows up on a phone.
Magazine
A separate premium Magazine refreshes itself from the site feed. The newsletter is rebuilt on a tested template library, with rendered proofs at phone and desktop width before every send.
Operations
Health checks run every six hours and the story feed refreshes every five minutes. One controller owns production changes, and every change keeps a rollback.
Social
A broadcast-graphics skill for show thumbnails, matchup cards and lower thirds. Stat graphics are checked against the final box score. It caught a touchdown-count error on September 26.
The agency
Eight agents, a production studio and a 24/7 automation layer, built white-label so any brand can drop in its own voice. That's how Back Porch runs in porch colors, not ours.

Quick fixes for Ronnie's side

Things we'd fix this week.

We found these on the public sites and profiles this morning. Each one is small, and each one costs reach every day it stays.

Auburn Undercover · Instagram

The bio still says 247Sports.

With 5,022 followers, it's the biggest account on Ronnie's side. It still tags @Auburn247 and #247Sports and links to auburn.247sports.com, even though Auburn Undercover went independent this summer.

Point it at auburnundercover.com and the 75-percent-off membership offer. It takes five minutes.

auburnundercover.com · phones

The site is locked at desktop width.

The page tells phones it is 1,090 pixels wide, so every reader on a phone pinches and zooms. Most football traffic arrives on phones.

We just did this exact repair at GatorBait. We'd scope what the platform allows and test it at 390px.

Auburn Undercover · X

Videos go out as bare links.

Recent posts share YouTube links "via @YouTube." Platforms bury off-site links, and it's the pattern our own playbook bans.

Post a native 30 to 60 second cut with captions and put the link in the reply. The studio makes the cut.

Back Porch Sports · everywhere

No Instagram, X or TikTok.

@backporchsports on Instagram and X belong to other, older shows. The Facebook page has 55 followers, and its latest post had one reaction.

Claim matching handles this week, then post clips from all six Saturday Sessions episodes on day one.

backporchsports.com

Finished work is switched off.

The message board, The Porch After Sundown, the YETI and Fanatics bands, Field Notes and Explore The Weekend are all built and hidden or disabled.

Turn each one on when there's something real behind it. The plan below puts a date on each.

Buddy Martin Show · YouTube

Our own channel still says Sidelines.

The Buddy Martin Show's channel description still calls it the Sidelines Live Network, so we have cleanup on our side too.

Rewrite it to point at GatorBait and the new SEC porch the day we announce.
